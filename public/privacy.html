POSEMATE PRIVACY COMMITMENT

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Introduction

PoseMate ("we", "our", or "the app") is a privacy-first camera overlay application built for photographers and creators. We believe your creative process and photos belong exclusively to you. PoseMate does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data or images.

2. Fully Offline Architecture

PoseMate operates entirely on-device and does not require an account, login, or internet connectivity to function:

  • No user tracking, profiling, or telemetry SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Meta Pixel).
  • No cloud image syncing or external servers.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

PoseMate requests only the permissions strictly required to perform its core photography workflow:

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely to display the live camera viewfinder and capture photographs. Camera frames are processed on-device in real time and are never recorded or streamed.
  • Photo Library Read Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used only when you tap "Choose from Photos" to select a reference pose image to superimpose over your camera.
  • Photo Library Write Access (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription): Used exclusively when you tap "Save Photo" to save your clean, captured image to your device camera roll.

4. Local Storage

The app uses local device storage (AsyncStorage) solely to store:

  • Local file references (URIs) of up to 5 recently selected reference images for convenient switching.
  • Basic on-device user preferences (e.g., onboarding dismissal flag).

This data remains on your physical device at all times and can be deleted simply by clearing recent references or uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Because PoseMate collects no personal information whatsoever, it is fully compliant with COPPA and does not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or PoseMate, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the project maintainers directly.
